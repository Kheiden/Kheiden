# Kurt Heiden

I'm building [Starlight Global Industries](https://github.com/Starlight-Global-Industries).

Starlight is AI that knows things. The physical kind, each traced to the pictures it came from.
Record a room or a shelf, and every item in it becomes a record you can check. Ask about any
item and the answer comes with its evidence.

- **Sluice**: record an area, and see each item it finds and the pictures it came from
- **Iris**: every item found in your recordings, with every picture of it and when it was seen
- **Streamline**: reviews what Sluice finds, merges duplicates, and asks only about what it could not settle
- **Exchange**: what each item is worth today, and every option for it
